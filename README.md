## About this entry

Most warning labels disclaim liability for things that might happen to the product. A time machine is the one product where the liability list itself becomes absurd: the manufacturer has to think about what happens when a customer runs into an earlier version of themselves, and then calmly itemise the fallout in the flat, unbothered language of a legal department. This entry leans into that deadpan register — a plain "do not meet your past self" instruction, followed by a liability waiver that starts with the expected physics problem, escalates to the bureaucratic problem of there suddenly being two of you, and only at the very end admits the manufacturer has also considered the domestic consequences. The joke is that the legal tone never cracks, even as the list quietly stops being about physics at all.
